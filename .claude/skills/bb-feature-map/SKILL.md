# Book Builder: where everything lives

File paths are relative to `src/`. If a file named here has moved, search for its main export
before assuming the feature is gone.

## The domain in one paragraph

A **series** holds books in a reading order, and a book can stand alone instead.
A **book** holds **parts** in order. A part either holds **chapters** (`has_chapters = 1`,
labelled "Part One", "Part Two", ...) or holds its own **story text** (a prologue, an
interlude). Chapters are numbered across the whole book. Each chapter or story-text part has
a **status**: idea, outlined, drafting, revising or done. **Characters**, **places** and
**systems** (magic, politics, trade) are the **story bible**: they belong to the user, can be
linked to many books, and are called **entries** in the code. A chapter's or part's **cast** is
the entries in it. **Mentions** are links from story text to entries, added by typing @; in the editor, the bible's names also link themselves wherever they're written, without being saved. **Footnotes** are notes tied to a place in story text, numbered by chapter.
Descriptions can show as subtitles in reading mode (`show_description`). **Headings** are how a
part or chapter opens in reading mode: a label and a title, either of which can be hidden.

More features, kept out of the table so it stays readable:

- **Version history** (the History button on the writing surface): `lib/components/story/VersionHistory.svelte` is the window (a list by day, a version's changes since or its text, Keep for good, Restore), and `StorySurface` shows the "Brought back" notice with its Undo. The rules for when a version is kept and how old ones thin out are in `lib/story-versions.ts` (no imports); the comparison is `lib/diff.ts` (no imports), worked out in the browser. Server: `keepVersion` in `lib/server/story-versions.ts`, called by every story-text save that changes the text, and `storyVersionHandlers` for `/api/story-text/{part,chapter}/[id]/versions` (GET list, POST keep the text now) and `/versions/[versionId]` (GET with its text, PUT `{ label }`, `null` to stop keeping). Restoring is `POST /versions/[versionId]/restore`, the `RESTORE` handler in `lib/server/story-text.ts`, because it's a save; `StoryTextDoc.restore` calls it and saves unsaved typing first. Table `story_version`. Why a version is kept when it is, and what it costs, is in `ideas/01-version-history.md` in the `bb-roadmap` skill.
- **Insights** (`/book/[id]/insights`, owner only): `lib/insights.ts` does the counting and has no imports, so it can be tested on its own: pacing, word lists, who's present where, and the "Worth a look" findings, with its thresholds as constants at the top. `lib/server/insights.ts` (`manuscriptInsights`) loads the book, puts it in reading order with `bookOutline` and hands it over. The page is `lib/components/insights/`: `InsightsPage`, `PacingChart`, `RepeatList`, `PresenceGrid`, `SectionTrack` (the row of chapter cells both of those last two use), `ChartTip`, `Segmented` and `sections.ts`. Nothing is stored and there's no table: it's worked out on each load. An entry counts as present through a mention, its name in plain text or a place in the cast, so a book written before mentions still works. The links in are on the book page and the outline.
- **Word maps** (`/book/[id]/word-map`, owner only): `lib/speakers.ts` works out who says each line, from the names in the story bible and with no list of speech verbs (its rules, how they were measured and their limits are in `ideas/17-word-maps.md` in the `bb-roadmap` skill). `lib/word-map.ts` tallies the whole book and each speaker through the same `countWord` as Insights, so a count means the same on both, and `wordMaps` in `lib/server/insights.ts` hands it the book. People are tallied by name with `countName`, apart from the words: stems would file Will with "willing" and Mark with "marked", and the small-word list would drop Will and May altogether. `withEveryName` adds whatever the story's @ links call a character, and shortens aliases as names are, so anyone in the bible or linked who appears in the text is pinned to the book's cloud however rarely they're named. `lib/word-cloud.ts` sets out the cloud: pure, with the measuring passed in, so it can be tested without a browser. The page is in `lib/components/insights/`: `WordMapPage`, `WordMapPanel` (one map: cloud, list and open word), `WordCloud` (lays out in the browser once the fonts arrive, arrow keys between words), `SpeakerList`, `UnplacedLines`, and `WordDetail`, which `RepeatList` shares. Nothing is stored: it's worked out on each load. The links in are on the book page and the Insights page.
- **Series** (`/series/[id]`, `/series/[id]/bible`, and `/author/[handle]/series/[id]` in public): `lib/components/series/` (`SeriesPage` with the reading order, `SeriesBible`, `PublicSeries`, `SeriesCovers`), `lib/components/book/BookSeries.svelte` on the book aside, rules shared with the browser in `lib/series.ts`, server in `lib/server/series.ts`. `/api/series` is GET and POST, `/api/series/[id]` is PUT and DELETE, and `PUT /api/series/[id]/books` takes `{ bookIds }`, the whole membership in order, so adding, removing and reordering are one call (`setSeriesBooks` in `lib/library.ts`, which updates the store first and goes back if the save fails). `seriesStore` holds the series. Reading mode gets its line and next book from `readerSeries`. The `@` menu treats the other books in a series as nearby (`family` in `searchEntries`). The order is dragged with `outline/drag.ts`; `locate` must return an object, because that file skips a falsy place.
- **Story bible** (`/book/[id]/bible`, and `/bible/[type]/[id]` for one entry, where type is `character`, `place` or `system`): `lib/components/bible/` (`BiblePage`, `BibleShelf` for the search, filters and cards a book and a series share, `EntryPage`, `EntrySheet`, `EntryAppearances`, `EntryConnections`, `Monogram`), shared rules and types in `lib/bible.ts` (no imports), server in `lib/server/bible.ts`. Each type's API is `/api/characters`, `/api/places` and `/api/systems`, with `[id]` for PUT and DELETE, all from the handlers in `lib/server/bible.ts`. Connections are `/api/bible/links`, drawn as a web on the connections map below. Where an entry appears (`appearancesOf`, and `appearanceCounts` for the cards) counts @ mentions from the `story_mention` index, names written out (worked out on each load by `namesWritten`), and casts. Stores: `charactersStore`, `placesStore`, `systemsStore`, and `entryStores[type]`.
- **Connections map** (`/book/[id]/bible/connections` and `/series/[id]/bible/connections`, owner only): the bible's connections drawn as a web. `lib/connections.ts` sorts a connection into a kind by its label (`relationKind`) and lays out the web (`layoutWeb`, `curve`, `bends`); it imports types only. The page is `lib/components/bible/ConnectionsPage.svelte` (scope, search, the kinds as filters, Map or List), with `ConnectionsMap` (pan, zoom, pinch, drag, arrow keys, Tidy up, the arrangement kept in `localStorage`), `ConnectionsPanel` (the chosen entry, or how to read the map and who isn't on it) and `ConnectionsList`. The panel reuses `EntryConnections`, which takes `onadd`, `onremove`, `onpick` and `mapHref` for this. The server only loads the connections, through `connectionsIn` in `lib/server/bible.ts`. The links in are the Connections button on both story bibles and "See the map" on an entry's page. How the web is laid out and kept is in `ideas/19-connections-map.md` in the `bb-roadmap` skill.
- **Mentions**: the node and the suggestion are in `lib/components/rich/mention.ts`, the `@` list is `MentionMenu.svelte`, the writer's hover card is `MentionCard.svelte`, and both are mounted by `RichEditor` when it's given a `bookId`. That editor also links the book's bible names wherever they're written, in the browser and unsaved (`rich/auto-mention.ts`, matching with `nameFinder` in `lib/bible.ts`). What a mention is, and how it's stored and cleaned, is in `lib/rich.ts`. Saving story text indexes them (`ownedMentions`, `indexMentions`). See the `bb-rich-text` skill.
- **Footnotes**: a node in story text carrying its note (a description value), cleaned, counted and turned into HTML in `lib/rich.ts` (`footnotesIn`, `textWithNotes`, `toHTML`'s third argument), with `storyWords` in `lib/reading.ts` counting the notes' words toward word counts and goals. In the editor: `lib/components/rich/footnote.ts` (the node, Ctrl+Alt+F, the toolbar's button in `Toolbar.svelte`) and `FootnoteBox.svelte`, the box the note is written in, mounted by `RichEditor` for story text. Reading mode lists the notes after each chapter and opens one on a tap with `lib/components/reader/NotePopover.svelte`, in both views. Exports: raised numbers and `storyOf`'s notes in `manuscript.ts`, notes at the foot of the page in `pdf.ts`, Word's own footnotes in `word.ts`. Version history compares with the notes in. No table and no API of its own: notes are saved with the story text. See the `bb-rich-text` skill, and `ideas/23-footnotes.md` in the `bb-roadmap` skill for the record.
- **Headings**: the rules and number formats are in `lib/headings.ts` (no runtime imports). A book sets its label styles and whether chapter titles show in `lib/components/book/BookHeadings.svelte` (book aside, `PUT /api/book/[id]/headings`). Each part or chapter sets its own label and title in `lib/components/story/HeadingFields.svelte`, used by `TitleBlock` (Edit details) and the outline's `Inspector`, saved through the usual part and chapter PUTs. `bookOutline()` numbers only what's on an automatic label, and its `label` is what the writing pages show, whatever the book's style. Reading mode and the book page get what readers see from `partHeading` and `chapterHeading`. `readableBook` blanks hidden titles, so test for a blank title, not a flag. Don't use `entry.label` to mean a part has a label: it's only null for story-text parts, and it's how much of the app tells the two kinds apart.
- **Casts**: a chapter's or story-text part's characters, places and systems. They're chosen in the outline's details panel (`Inspector`), shown on its rows by `CastStack`, and filtered by its "Whole cast" menu. The server reaches all six tables through `castTable` in `lib/server/bible.ts`. `PUT /api/cast/[target]/[id]` takes `{ type, ids }` and replaces that one type. Casts also fill by themselves: a first @ mention (`indexMentions`) or a name from the book's story bible written for the first time (`castNamed` on save, `castByName` when an entry joins a book or changes its names) adds the entry.
- **Export**: `GET /api/book/[id]/export` and `/api/part/[partId]/export`, with `?format=pdf` or `docx`, owner only. Their `config` gives them their own Vercel function with a 60s limit, which keeps the typefaces out of the main one. `lib/components/book/ExportButtons.svelte` is the buttons, used by `BookAside` and `PartPage`, and it saves unsaved text first. `lib/server/export/manuscript.ts` builds what goes in from `readableBook`, so a file holds what reading mode shows and nothing else. `pdf.ts` sets it as a 6×9 in book with PDFKit and our own line breaking, and `fonts.ts` loads the `.woff` files in `fonts/` (kept with their licences) and picks a face for each character. `word.ts` writes a Letter document with real Word styles using `docx`. `lib/export.ts` has the formats and file names, and `lib/cover.ts` the cover design shared with `BookCover`. How the typesetting works, and what to change when story text gains something new, is the `bb-export` skill.
- **Accounts and email**: new accounts confirm their email before anything opens. `lib/server/auth.ts` sets Better Auth to `requireEmailVerification`, with no sending on sign-up or sign-in of its own: the log-in actions call `sendConfirmation` (`lib/server/account.ts`) so a failed send reaches the page (`/verify?unsent`). `hooks.server.ts` also ignores any session of an unconfirmed account. `/verify` is "check your inbox", Send it again, and where the link lands (`?confirmed`, Better Auth adds `&error=`); the address waiting is in the `bb-pending-email` cookie. `/forgot-password` asks for the link and never says whether an account exists; `/reset-password` takes the token Better Auth hands on, saves (signing out every other device), marks the email confirmed (`onPasswordReset`) and logs in. The four pages share `lib/components/auth/AuthShell.svelte`, the brand panel and form styles the log-in page had. Email: `lib/server/email.ts` posts to Resend's API with `fetch` (no package), limits sends per address and visitor with rows in the `verification` table (`bb-email:*`), and prints to the terminal in dev without `RESEND_API_KEY`; `lib/server/emails.ts` is the two emails, tables and inline styles, with `static/email/mark.png` as the logo. Deleting an account: the Delete your account card on Settings opens `lib/components/profile/DeleteAccount.svelte` (what goes, counted from the stores; the phrase `ACCOUNT_PHRASE` in `lib/confirm.ts` and the password), then `DELETE /api/account` calls Better Auth's `deleteUser`, and everything cascades from the user row, landing on `/login?deleted`.
- **Ambient sound**: `lib/ambient.ts` makes the sounds and mixes them (`AmbientMixer`), all Web Audio with no files. `lib/components/story/FocusSound.svelte` is the button and menu in focus mode's bar, remembered in `bb-focus-sound`. A change to a recipe needs measuring (see the `bb-verify` skill).

## How data reaches pages

- **App pages.** Book, part and chapter pages mostly read the client stores (`lib/stores`), filled by `loadLibrary()` from the `/api/*` list endpoints, and write through `sendJSON` / `putJSON`. After a write they update the stores in place rather than reloading.
- **Server-loaded pages.** Reading mode, profiles and the author page load on the server in `+page.server.ts` and pass plain data to one component.
- **Ordering** always goes through `bookOutline()` in `lib/library.ts`, which sorts by position, then created date, then id.

## Reading position, shared by both reader views

- **Format.** `{ key: 'chapter-12' | 'part-3', fraction }` is kept in `localStorage` under `bb-reading-<bookId>`.
- **Who writes it.** The scroll view measures it against a reading line, and the book view uses the page's column within its chapter. Switching views passes the position across.
- **Preferences.** Text size, typeface and view live in the `bb-reader` cookie (`3-sans-book`), so the server renders the right one.

## What isn't here yet

- **Planned.** The `bb-roadmap` skill lists what's planned, and a plan for each of the next ideas
  (uploaded artwork, pictures in story text, the art studio, a cover designer and more). If a task
  sounds like a new feature, look there before designing it from nothing.
- **No files.** Nothing is uploaded or stored outside Postgres yet. Covers are generated from the
  title (`lib/cover.ts`, `ui/BookCover.svelte`) and portraits are initials (`bible/Monogram`).
- **Coming soon.** The homepage's "Coming soon" shelf (`lib/components/marketing/soon.ts`) lists
  what visitors are told is coming. When you build one of those features, take it off the shelf
  and consider showing it on the homepage (see the `bb-homepage` skill).
